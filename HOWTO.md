# How to use Dynastik ?

It's pretty simple (at least, I hope so...) !

Just follow these instructions to get started !

### 1. Create your svg

First, you're going to need a svg.

You need a svg file with some placeholder text instead of the dynamic text.

If you are using Figma, disable the option "Outline text" in the options.

### 2. Edit your svg

Open the svg on in your favorite text editor, and find the text you want to replace.

Replace it with `{!}replaceme{!}`, then copy the content and move to the next step

### 3. Generate your link

Open [the generator](https://dynastik.vercel.app) and choose the thing you would like to track.

Then, fill the form and paste your SVG in the svg field.

Finally, hit enter ! Now, you have a veeeeery long url that you can use anywhere !
